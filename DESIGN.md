# Finish Beacon design direction

## Subject, audience, and job

Mobile detailing for Houston drivers who want the cabin, wheels, glass, and paint treated as connected parts of one vehicle. The page should make that whole-vehicle view clear and lead to calling or booking once contact details are confirmed.

## Visual system

- Deep signal — `#11202C`
- Beacon blue — `#203B52`
- Ceramic — `#E7ECEA`
- White light — `#FAFCFB`
- Signal cyan — `#4CB7C5`
- Beacon copper — `#D98B52`
- Display: Archivo Black
- Body: Manrope
- Utility: IBM Plex Mono

## Layout thesis

Reuse the editorial/service-process frame and convert the hero curve into a single beacon sweep connecting cabin, wheels, glass, and bodywork.

## Signature

One asymmetric beacon sweep passes through four service touchpoints and directs attention to the whole vehicle.

## Critique

The first concept used too many lighting motifs. The revision keeps one functional sweep on a ceramic-and-blue automotive surface.
